Craft & Heritage

One Life, One Craft — the Universe in Miniature

Decades of wood-carving mastery in Dongyang — China's “hometown of wood carving” — and more than ten years of dedicated pit-carving in Suzhou's pit-carving village: Master Hua devotes himself entirely to the olive pit, condensing the grand discipline of sculpture into a fruit pit no larger than a thumbnail. Every piece receives museum-grade rigour: rare materials, hand-made micro-blades, and the most precious ingredient of all — time.

01

Selecting the Pit

Pits are hand-picked from mature olive groves and aged for years. Density, symmetry and grain are the only criteria — fewer than one in a hundred earns the chisel.

02

Composing & Innovating

The design is drawn directly onto a pit barely three centimetres long. Building on tradition, Master Hua breaks free from conventional Buddhist and Taoist subjects to open entirely new territory: from peonies and cabbage, plum, orchid, bamboo and chrysanthemum, to birds, cicadas, crabs and toads, the twelve zodiac animals, even pavilions, boats, vases and birdcages — each rendered with a fineness and clarity beyond traditional pit-carving.

03

The Blade

Extraordinary fineness and inventive expression demand extreme tools. Master Hua grinds ordinary sewing needles into bespoke micro-chisels, one by one, by hand. Only blades this fine can move freely through relief a tenth of a millimetre deep — giving a lifetime of mastery its fullest expression.

04

Honours

With consummate skill and a style entirely his own, Master Hua's olive-pit carvings have repeatedly represented the craft at national arts-and-crafts exhibitions across China — winning gold medals and the attention of collectors and connoisseurs alike.

Care & Wisdom

Maintenance & FAQ

Five Rules of Care

A well-kept carving deepens in colour for decades. Five habits are all it takes.

01
Keep it dry

Brief contact with water is tolerable; soaking is not. If caught in rain, pat dry and air-rest.

02
Oil, lightly

A trace of neutral oil — olive or jojoba — every two to three months nourishes the pit and deepens its patina.

03
Brush, gently

A soft dry brush lifts dust from the deepest relief. Never use chemicals or metal polish.

04
Store with care

Rest in the included cloth pouch, away from radiators, air-conditioning vents and strong sunlight.

05
Wear it often

Skin contact is the finest finish of all — years of wear turn the pit a translucent amber.

Common Questions

Everything collectors usually ask before their first piece.

Are the pieces waterproof?

Olive pits are naturally dense and oily, and our carvings tolerate brief contact with water — but they are not waterproof. Do not soak them, wear them in the shower or sauna, or clean them in an ultrasonic bath. If a piece gets wet, pat it dry and let it rest in the open air, away from direct heat.

How should I care for my carving over time?

Brush dust from the crevices with a soft, dry brush. Every two to three months, apply a trace of neutral oil with a fingertip, let it absorb, then buff with a soft cloth. Regular wearing builds a warm amber patina — this is the piece ageing beautifully, not damage.

Do you take custom commissions?

Yes. Portrait busts, zodiac animals, Buddhist figures, commemorative pieces — most subjects can be carved. Commissions take four to eight weeks and begin with a 50% deposit. Send a description or reference image through the inquiry form and we will reply with a sketch and quotation.

How is shipping handled?

Every piece ships worldwide by EMS, DHL or FedEx, fully insured, with tracking and an export certificate. Packaging is custom-fitted and climate-buffered. Delivery typically takes five to ten business days, and we handle all customs paperwork.

Is each piece truly unique?

Yes — completely. Every carving is made from a natural olive pit; no two pits, and no two compositions, are alike. Each piece arrives with a certificate of authenticity signed by the carver, stating the piece number, subject and date of completion.

Do you offer wholesale or gallery partnerships?

We do. For galleries, boutiques and collectors' shops we offer wholesale pricing, a seasonal line sheet and private-label options. Mention “WHOLESALE” in your message and our partnership desk will reply within one business day.
